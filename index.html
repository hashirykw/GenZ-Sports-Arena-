Our Story

More Than a Court

Gen Z Sports Arena was built on a single idea — Karachi deserved a padel venue that takes the game seriously without taking itself too seriously. Tucked into Block H, North Nazimabad, we've become the city's go-to destination for casual rallies to high-energy tournaments.

Our staff are players first. They'll sort your equipment, coach you through your first rally, or simply cheer you on courtside. Grab a bite from our food counter, shower up after a hard match, and head home feeling like you've found your people.
